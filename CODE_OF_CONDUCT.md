# Code of conduct

Cloudmorrow is built by people who want to keep their own data and help
others do the same. The places where that happens, the issues, pull
requests, discussions and chat rooms of this organisation, are for anyone
who shares that aim, whoever they are.

## The rule

Treat people here the way you would treat someone who walked into your home
to help you fix something: with patience, honesty and respect. Argue about
the code, the design and the words as hard as you like. Never about the
person.

That means, concretely:

- Assume the other person means well, and say so when you are unsure.
- Criticise work, not people, and say what would be better.
- No insults, harassment, slurs, sexualised remarks, or publishing of
  anybody's private details, here or anywhere else about someone met here.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Everyone is, often.
- Newcomers get the same answer an expert would, written so they can follow it.

## When it goes wrong

If someone makes you or anyone else unwelcome, write to the maintainer
privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/jimmibram) or in a private vulnerability report
if the matter is sensitive. Every report is read, kept confidential, and
acted on, which can mean a quiet word, an edited or removed comment, or
being asked to leave.

The maintainer holds the same standard and is accountable to it.
