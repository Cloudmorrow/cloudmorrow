own your data, choose your apps

The look the app, the cloudmorrow command and the terminal app share, from the brand guide: the app's blue-black glass, the blue of the cloud for the platform and the amber of the hedgehog for you. Pixels for the marks, real type for the words: the wordmark and the headings below are drawn dot by dot, and everything you actually read is not.

Where it lives

  1. src/cloudmorrow/palette.py for the CLI and the terminal app: the Textual theme in tui/theme.py and every CLI status line read from it.
  2. The :root of src/cloudmorrow/server/web/base.css for the phone and the full web app, with the rules for what is blue and what is amber written above it.
  3. The brand guide itself, with the logo, the hedgehog, the voice and the website, is in the cloudmorrow-web repository: brand/index.html and brand/tokens.css.
