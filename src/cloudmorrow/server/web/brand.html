own your data, choose your apps

Three ways the whole thing could look — the app on the phone, the cloudmorrow command, and the terminal app, all out of one set of twelve colours. Pixels for the marks, real type for the words: the wordmark and the headings below are drawn dot by dot, and everything you actually read is not.

If you pick one

  1. Paste the palette.py block over src/cloudmorrow/palette.py. The Textual theme in tui/theme.py and every CLI status line already read from it, so both change at once.
  2. Paste the base.css block over the :root in src/cloudmorrow/server/web/base.css, and raise --radius — that one value is most of what makes the app look square today.
  3. The new GRADIENT tuple is for logo.py: tint the block letters across it a character at a time instead of the two flat tones it uses now.
  4. The 5×7 font that draws the marks lives in brand.js for the moment. If a theme wins, it moves into core.js beside the eleven letters that are already there, and the headings on the phone can be drawn with it too.
